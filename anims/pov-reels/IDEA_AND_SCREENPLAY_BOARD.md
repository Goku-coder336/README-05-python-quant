## How we'd make one (same workflow as before)
1. **Pick an idea.** I write the exact picture prompts (room + character sheets + 8–10 poses).
2. **Pictures:** Kling or ChatGPT/Gemini (pictures are cheap; about 10 needed).
3. **Animation + edit (free, done by me):** pose swaps on the beat, bounces, zoom-punches, shake, slash/bonk, POV caption,
   meme sound effects (ElevenLabs).
4. **Music:** add a trending Malayalam/Tamil song in Instagram, or I build one in from a song you send.
