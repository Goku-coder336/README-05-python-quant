# Blue Saree: motion prompts for your 10 pictures

Your pictures, in story order (saved in `assets/blue-saree/stills/`). For each one: upload the picture in an
**image-to-video** app as the **first frame**, paste the prompt, make **5 seconds** at **9:16**, and save it as
`shot01.mp4` … `shot10.mp4`.

**Add this to every prompt:**
`Cinematic, keep both faces and outfits exactly as in the image, no morphing, natural smooth motion, no text.`
**Negative prompt (if the app has one):** `morphing face, extra fingers, distorted hands, flicker, text, watermark`

**Free apps:** **Kling AI** for cloth, water and walking; **Hailuo AI** for faces and expressions. Each gives free daily
credits, so do 2–4 shots a day, hero shots (★) first. Meta AI's "Animate" is a free backup for any shot.

**01: Waiting out the rain**
`He rests his chin on his hand and gazes at the rain with a soft dreamy smile, then blinks slowly. Heavy rain pours off
the tin roof edge in streams, puddles ripple on the cobblestones, steam curls from the kettle, the hanging bulb sways
slightly, people with umbrellas walk past in the background. Slow dolly push in.`

**02: She runs in ★**
`She runs into the shelter laughing, holding up her wet blue saree, her braid swinging, water splashing under her sandals,
heavy rain pouring behind her. The man at the counter slowly turns his head to watch her. Camera tracks with her, slight
slow motion.`

**03: Same glass of chai ★**
`Two hands slowly reach toward the glass of chai from both sides and stop a hair apart, fingertips hesitating. The gold
bangles shimmer, steam curls up from the tea, rain falls behind. Static macro shot with a gentle rack focus onto the hands.`

**04: He's struck ★**
`He looks at her, completely still, his eyes slowly widen and his lips part as if to speak, but he says nothing. In the
blurred foreground her braid and jasmine sway slightly. Steam rises from the chai, rain behind. Very slow push in on his face.`

**05: She smiles back ★**
`Resting her cheek on her hand, she smiles shyly, glances down, then looks back up at him. Her jhumka earrings sway and
wet strands of hair move gently. Steam rises from the glass, rain falls behind her. Slow push in.`

**06: The rain stops**
`Golden sunset over the Chinese fishing nets. The great nets slowly dip toward the water, gentle waves wash over the
rocks, a small boat drifts, birds glide across the sky, leaves sway in the breeze in the foreground. Slow crane down
from the leaves to the sea.`

**07: Together ★**
`They walk slowly side by side along the wet seaside promenade at sunset. He pushes his old bicycle, the wheels turning.
They glance at each other and smile. Her saree end and hair move in the sea breeze, the street lamps glow. Camera moves
backward in front of them.`

**08: The gift**
`He holds out a single jasmine flower. She smiles, tilts her head on her hand and looks at him. Their hair moves in the
sea breeze, the sun sparkles on the water and the fishing nets sway behind them. Slow push in.`

**09: That night**
`Festival night. She looks up at him and smiles shyly, he looks down at her and smiles back. Oil lamp flames flicker,
lanterns sway gently, glowing embers float up, people move softly in the blurred background. Slow push in.`

**10: Ending ★**
`She closes her eyes and smiles warmly as he holds the jasmine flower toward her. Her hair moves gently, lamp flames
flicker, jasmine petals drift down from the branches above. Very slow push in, ending on her smile.`

## If a clip goes wrong
- **Face changes:** make it again with *"subtle motion, keep the face unchanged"* added, or use Hailuo instead.
- **Too much movement:** add *"gentle, minimal movement"*.
- **Hands melt (shot 03):** add *"hands move only slightly"*.
- **Out of credits:** send me the picture anyway and I'll give that shot camera motion, rain, steam and light myself.
  One or two of these mixed in with real AI clips won't stand out.

## What I do when you send the clips
Put them in story order, trim each to its best ~3 s, match the colours across all 10, add soft transitions, light leaks
and film grain, and time the cuts to *Nenchaadiye* (send me a screen recording of the 30 s you'll use).
Then export the 9:16 reel; you add the song in Instagram.
