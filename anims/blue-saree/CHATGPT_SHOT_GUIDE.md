# Blue Saree: ChatGPT version (picture for each shot, plus a Sora motion prompt)

**How to use**
1. Open **one new ChatGPT chat** and keep the whole project in it. ChatGPT remembers the characters inside one chat.
2. Paste **Message 1** (setup). Then **Message 2** (Meera) and **Message 3** (Arjun). Save the pictures you like.
3. For each shot, paste that shot's **ChatGPT message**. If a face drifts, reply:
   *"Keep Meera's face exactly like the character sheet above and regenerate."*
4. Download each picture as `shot01.png`, `shot02.png`…
5. **Motion:** paste the shot's **Sora prompt** into Sora (sora.com, or the video option in ChatGPT if your plan has it),
   with the picture uploaded as the starting image. Save as `shot01.mp4`…
   *Free ChatGPT accounts get only a few images a day, and Sora video may need a paid plan. If you can't make video,
   just send me the pictures and I'll animate them (rain, steam, light, camera moves).*
6. Upload everything here and I'll edit the reel. You add *Nenchaadiye* in Instagram.

## The shots

### Shot 1: Rain on the street (0.0–2.7 s)
**ChatGPT:**
```
Shot 1. Wide shot of a rainy afternoon on a narrow Fort Kochi street with old colonial shopfronts. A small tea stall
with a rusty tin awning. ARJUN stands under the awning beside an old black bicycle, looking out at the rain. A glass of
chai steams on the wooden counter. Puddles reflect warm shop lights. Calm, a little lonely.
```
**Sora:** `Heavy rain falls, raindrops ripple in the puddles, water drips from the awning edge, steam rises from the tea,
the man breathes calmly and glances down the street. Slow dolly push-in. Keep his face and clothes the same. 5 seconds.`

### Shot 2: She runs in (2.7–5.4 s) ★
**ChatGPT:**
```
Shot 2. Medium shot. MEERA runs in under the same tea-stall awning to escape the rain, laughing, shaking water off the
loose end of her blue saree. Raindrops on her face and hair. ARJUN is partly visible at the right edge, turning to look at her.
```
**Sora:** `The woman slows down under the awning and shakes the end of her saree, water droplets fly off the fabric, her
braid swings, rain pours behind her, the man turns his head toward her. Smooth handheld tracking shot. 5 seconds.`

### Shot 3: Same glass of chai (5.4–8.1 s)
**ChatGPT:**
```
Shot 3. Close-up of two hands reaching for the same last glass of chai on a wet wooden counter, fingers almost
touching. Her hand has thin gold bangles; his comes from a cream rolled sleeve. Steam curls up. Rain-blurred street
lights behind. Shallow depth of field. Make the hands and fingers anatomically correct.
```
**Sora:** `Both hands slowly move toward the glass and stop just before touching, the bangles shimmer, steam curls upward,
focus shifts from the glass to the hands. Static camera. 5 seconds.`

## Fixes for common ChatGPT problems
| Problem | Reply in the same chat |
|---|---|
| Came out square or landscape | "Make it vertical 9:16 portrait." |
| Face changed | "Keep Meera's (or Arjun's) face exactly like the character sheet above and regenerate." |
| Too realistic or photographic | "More painterly and animated, like a Pixar-Ghibli film still." |
| Text or a watermark appeared | "Remove all text and regenerate." |
| Hands look wrong (shot 3) | "Fix the hands: five natural fingers each, relaxed and anatomically correct." |
| Saree colour drifted | "The saree must be royal blue with a thin gold border." |
| Daily limit reached | Continue tomorrow **in the same chat**, so it still remembers the characters. |

**Order:** character sheets, then ★ shots 8, 2, 4, 5, then the rest.
