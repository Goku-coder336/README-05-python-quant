# "POV: Amma asks me to help 🙂": storyboard and screenplay
Style: retro lo-fi anime (same as the reference reel), indigo/beige kitchen, 9:16, about 26 s.
Music: *Killin' 'em* ("gotta put 'em in the dirt") for each strut, *Aeroplane* for the ending (both from *Scene*).
Voice: Amma in Tamil (ElevenLabs). Plan **B**: 4 Kling clips + 3 pictures animated in the edit.

## Cast
- **Amma:** calm Tamil mom, maroon saree with a mustard border, black blouse, bun with jasmine, steel ladle.
- **Kutty:** chibi anime boy, spiky hair, thick black square glasses, sleepy eyes, red T-shirt, navy shorts, pink swirl lollipop.
- **Set:** the same indigo/beige kitchen in every shot: blue fridge with notes, blue floor tiles, tube light, ceiling fan,
  steel vessels, doorway to the hall.

---

## Screenplay

**INT. KITCHEN, EVENING.** Amma is cooking alone, pots everywhere. She's tired.

> **AMMA** (sweetly, over her shoulder): "Kutty… konjam help pannuriya?"
> *(Kutty, can you help me a little?)*

Kutty strolls past with a lollipop, hands in his pockets, eyes half closed. He doesn't even look at her.
🎵 *"Gotta put 'em in the dirt…"*

**At the sink.** A mountain of vessels.

> **AMMA** (a little louder): "Kutty, paathiram kazhuvi tharriya?"
> *(Kutty, will you wash the vessels?)*

Kutty struts the other way. Same walk. Same lollipop. 🎵 *"Gotta put 'em in the dirt…"*

**At the clothes line.** A heavy bucket of wet clothes.

> **AMMA** (strained): "Kutty… thuni kaaya podu da!"
> *(Kutty… hang the clothes, da!)*

Kutty struts past again, now twirling the lollipop. 🎵 *"Gotta put 'em in the dirt…"*

The music stops dead. Amma slowly turns. Anime death stare.

> **AMMA** (low, then exploding): "Kutty… KUTTYYYY!!"

⚡ **GOD MODE.** The lights flicker. A glowing aura, floating hair, glowing eyes, the saree flying.

Amma lifts Kutty above her head and spins him like a plane. The lollipop is still in his mouth.
🎵 ***"Aeroplane!"***

**FADE OUT.**
