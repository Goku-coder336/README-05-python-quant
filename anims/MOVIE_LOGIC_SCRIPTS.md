# Movie Logic skits: script list

Format (from the reference reel): two crude line-drawn characters on a film set, an ACTOR stuck
in a movie cliché and a deadpan DIRECTOR with a megaphone. One caption line at a time,
hard cuts between speakers, 12-25 s, one cliché per video, a visual payoff at the end.

Build any of these: write `anims/<name>/script.json`, run `python3 anims/tools/voice.py anims/<name>`,
copy `anims/bomb-timer.html` and swap the props/payoff. The characters live in `anims/shared/meme-cast.js`.

## 1. The Bomb Timer  (BUILT: assets/clips/bomb-timer.mp4)
Bomb ticks 0:03, 0:02, 0:01... snip, it stops at one second.
ACTOR: Why does it ALWAYS stop at one second?
DIRECTOR: Drama.
ACTOR: Why does a bomb even have a timer?
DIRECTOR: So you know how long you've got.
ACTOR: The bomber WANTED me to know?
DIRECTOR: He's considerate.
ACTOR: So he's evil... but considerate?
DIRECTOR: He's a complex character.
(beep... beep-beep... BOOM. Actor is charred with an afro.)
DIRECTOR: ...Okay. That's lunch.

## 2. Cool Guys Don't Look at Explosions
Actor walks toward camera, a huge explosion behind him.
ACTOR: Can I turn around?
DIRECTOR: Cool guys don't look at explosions.
ACTOR: My back is literally on fire.
DIRECTOR: Coolly on fire.
ACTOR: Can I at least walk faster?
DIRECTOR: Slow motion. Budget's paid for.
(Payoff: his eyebrows fall off, one at a time.)

## 3. Henchmen Attack One at a Time
Hero surrounded by eight henchmen, they wait their turn.
HENCHMAN: Why are we queueing?
DIRECTOR: Fight etiquette.
HENCHMAN: There's eight of us. He's one guy.
DIRECTOR: And he's got the script.
HENCHMAN: Can two of us go?
DIRECTOR: That's a sequel.
(Payoff: henchman takes a ticket from a deli-counter number machine: "Now serving: 7".)

## 4. Infinite Ammo
Actor has fired a six-shooter for a full minute.
ACTOR: I've fired 200 bullets from a six-shooter.
DIRECTOR: And?
ACTOR: It holds SIX.
DIRECTOR: Reloading is boring.
ACTOR: So where are the bullets coming from?
DIRECTOR: Plot.
(Payoff: he opens the cylinder, a tiny writer inside is handing him bullets.)

## 5. "I'm In." (Hacker Scene)
Actor smashes a keyboard for 3 seconds.
ACTOR: I'm in.
DIRECTOR: Perfect.
ACTOR: I typed "asdfjkl".
DIRECTOR: Hacked the Pentagon.
ACTOR: The screen just turned green.
DIRECTOR: Green means hacked.
(Payoff: printer prints "ACCESS GRANTED" on one sheet, very slowly.)

## 6. Enhance!
Blurry CCTV still on a monitor.
DIRECTOR: Enhance.
ACTOR: It's four pixels.
DIRECTOR: Enhance.
ACTOR: Now it's four BIGGER pixels.
DIRECTOR: Enhance!
(Payoff: zooms into a reflection in an eyeball, and it's the director holding a megaphone.)

## 7. The Villain Monologue
Hero tied to a chair, villain has the gun.
HERO: Why don't you just shoot me?
VILLAIN: First, I explain my entire plan.
HERO: That took 40 minutes.
VILLAIN: It's a complex plan.
DIRECTOR: Cut! He escapes now.
VILLAIN: How?
DIRECTOR: You tied him with one rope.
(Payoff: the rope is a shoelace.)

## 8. The Phone Call That Never Says Goodbye
ACTOR (on the phone): Meet me at the docks.
(hangs up)
ACTOR: I didn't say bye. Or where. Or which docks.
DIRECTOR: He'll know.
ACTOR: There are 400 docks.
DIRECTOR: He'll know.
(Payoff: the other guy is already standing right behind him.)

## 9. Horror Movie: Let's Split Up
ACTOR: There's a killer in the house. Why are we splitting up?
DIRECTOR: Script says so.
ACTOR: Can we go together?
DIRECTOR: He needs you alone.
ACTOR: Can he come to both of us?
DIRECTOR: He's union. Fifteen-minute breaks.
(Payoff: the killer is sitting on a crate behind them eating a sandwich.)

## 10. Perfect Hair After Waking Up
Actor wakes in bed with flawless hair and full makeup.
ACTOR: Why do I wake up with perfect hair?
DIRECTOR: You're the lead.
ACTOR: The guy next to me looks like a broom.
DIRECTOR: He's an extra.
(Payoff: the extra wakes up and his hair falls off.)

## 11. Car Explodes From a Fender Bender
ACTOR: I tapped a lamppost at 5 miles an hour.
DIRECTOR: Mm-hm.
ACTOR: The car exploded twice.
DIRECTOR: Movie cars run on gunpowder.
ACTOR: What does the car run on in real life?
DIRECTOR: ...Also gunpowder.

## 12. The Doctor Line
DOCTOR: We did everything we could.
ACTOR: He sprained his ankle.
DOCTOR: Everything we could.
ACTOR: You gave him a plaster.
DIRECTOR: Say it slower. With the glasses off.
(Payoff: doctor removes glasses, there's another pair underneath.)
