Questions

Good to know

When does Festora open?

Kochi first. Waitlist members get in before everyone else and hear from us on launch day.

Is it free to plan?

Yes. Planning and comparing is free. You pay vendors only when you book.

Which traditions are covered?

Kerala Hindu, Christian and Muslim ceremonies, mixed and intimate events to start, with more added alongside local vendors.

How do rental deposits work?

The deposit is held when you book and released once the gear is back and checked.

Are vendors verified?

A verified badge appears only after we check ID, business details and past work.

I am a vendor. Can I list?

Yes, listing is free. Join the waitlist and tell us you are a vendor when we get in touch.
