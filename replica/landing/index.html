Questions

Before you join

When does Festora launch?

We open in Kochi first. Waitlist members get access before everyone else and we will message you on launch day.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Joining the waitlist and planning your event is free. You only pay vendors when you book.

Which traditions do you cover?

We start with Kerala Hindu, Christian and Muslim ceremonies plus mixed and intimate events. More are added with local vendors.

How do rental deposits work?

The deposit is held when you book and released after the gear is returned and checked. Photos are taken at handover and return.

Are vendors checked?

Every vendor shows a verified badge only after we check their ID, business details and past work.

Can I cancel a booking?

Each vendor shows its cancellation terms before you pay, so you always know what happens if plans change.

I am a vendor. How do I join?

Listing is free. Join the waitlist, choose "I am a vendor" when we message you, and we will help set up your profile.

Which cities come next?

Trivandrum and Calicut after Kochi, then more of South India and the UAE.
