# Brand: renaming from "Festora"

**Why:** Festora Events Ltd is an active UK events company (incorporated February 2026, Manchester), festora.com is taken, and other businesses trade as Festora. Same industry plus planned UK launch means a real risk of a dispute or forced rebrand later.

## Screening done (27 November 2026)
28 names checked by web search for an existing events or marketplace business with the same name.

**Ruled out** (already used): Utsavo (Indian event invitations platform and an events company), Utsavly (Indian celebration vendor marketplace), Mehfil (event and venue booking apps, India and Pakistan), Gatherwise (event planner software), Gathrly (event venue platform, UK company), Occasio (event rentals and productions), Thoranam (event decorators), Tamboo (Indian events company), Hostwell (rental management), Kalyo (psychology software), Occaro (Mamas & Papas pushchair, UK), Onecue (software tool), Selevo (HR software, medicine). Melam and Pandal are common event words and cannot be owned as a brand. Others cut on sight: Jalsa, Sabha, Kolam (very common), Evora (place), Lumora and Celebre (too close to Festora), Eventhaven and Plannet (generic).

**Shortlist** (no event business found by web search):

| Name | Idea | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Avasara** | Sanskrit and Hindi *avasar*, "occasion" | Meaningful across India; easy to say in English and Arabic |
| **Utsella** | *utsav* (celebration) with a soft ending | Warm, festive; invented, so easier to protect |
| **Pandalio** | *pandal*, the event canopy across South Asia | Ties to both events and gear rental; playful |
| **Ollavi** | Invented | Neutral and global; says nothing about events on its own |
| **Kalavo** | Invented, echoes *kala* (art) | Short, neutral |
| Ruvelo | Invented | Only unrelated uses found (shoes, a pet product, a template); weakest of the six |
