Barcelona won the 2014/15 Champions League, and the treble, in a season their new coach Luis Enrique
called "a transitional year". In the group stage, Lionel Messi scored 8 of their 15 goals. In the knockout
rounds he scored 2 of their 16, while Neymar and Suárez scored 12. This episode follows the goals round
by round, from Group F to the final in Berlin, and looks at where they went.

Run of the Champions follows every Champions League winner's full run, from the group stage to the final,
from 2014/15 to 2025/26. This is Episode 1.
