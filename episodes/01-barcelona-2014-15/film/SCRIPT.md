# Barcelona 2014/15: the goals moved

Run of the Champions, Episode 1, rebuilt as a HyperFrames film. 1920x1080, 30 fps, target 6:30 to 7:30.
Narration: ElevenLabs George (`eleven_multilingual_v2`), calm analyst read, one file per line; the timeline is
built from the measured durations. Captions on screen for every line. Every number has a source tag
(see `SOURCES.md`): [UEFA] official match pages and season statistics, [SB] StatsBomb open data for the final,
[W] Wikipedia's 2014/15 Champions League page (group table), [ESPN], [AP], [RFI].

## What changed from the earlier version (accuracy rules in this brief)

- **Messi as top assister (6) is out.** UEFA says 6; ESPN's stats page says 4 (Iniesta 5). The brief says to
  leave out anything sources disagree on.
- **Shot counts in the final are out** (StatsBomb 17 v 15, UEFA 18 v 14). xG is StatsBomb's own measure, so it
  stays, labelled "according to StatsBomb".
- **"290 on-ball actions" becomes "290 events in the data, the most of any Barcelona player"**: 290 is every
  StatsBomb event for Messi, including 6 pressures and 3 fouls.
- **No claims about why.** "He stopped being the finisher and became the supplier" is gone: no source says it,
  and StatsBomb credits Messi with 1 chance created in the final (Rakitić 4). The film shows what happened.
- **Player photos are out** (the brief bans them). Players are dots, name tags and comparison cards.
